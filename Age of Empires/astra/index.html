Villagers carry resources back to the town centre. Select a villager to build; right-click an unfinished building to help. Houses add 5 population. Farms grow food. Soldiers automatically fight nearby enemies.

Train at your town centre and barracks. Press the small × on a queue to cancel and refund. Advance through three ages at the town centre to improve gathering, spearmen and every watchtower. Watchtowers fire automatically and reveal distant land. Dark land is unexplored; dim land was explored but is currently out of sight. Enemy units disappear from view there, while buildings remain as last seen. Protect your town centre and destroy Redfen’s to win.
