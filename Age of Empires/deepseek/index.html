Verdant Vale

Raise a settlement, advance through the ages, and raze the rival town centre.

First steps

  1. Drag a box over your three villagers.
  2. Right-click trees and berry bushes to gather.
  3. Build a House, then a Barracks.
  4. Advance to the Keep Age from the town centre.

Controls

  • Left click / drag — select
  • Right click — move, gather, attack, help build
  • W A S D or screen edge — pan · wheel — zoom
  • H home · P pause · M mute · R restart

Fog of war is on: unexplored ground is black, land you have seen but cannot watch is dimmed. Scout out Ashfell and destroy their town centre to win.

Controls

Camera

  • W A S D or arrow keys — pan
  • Move mouse to screen edge — pan
  • Middle-drag — pan
  • Mouse wheel — zoom in / out
  • H — jump to town centre
  • Click the minimap — jump camera

Groups

  • Select 2+ units — formation buttons appear
  • Line / Box / Staggered / Flank — pick a shape, then move
  • Formations re-form the group on the spot

Commands

  • Left click — select a unit or building
  • Left drag — selection box
  • Shift + click — add to selection
  • Right click — move, gather, attack or build
  • Esc — cancel / clear selection
  • Right-click a farm — villagers work the field
  • P pause · M mute · R restart

Ages

Select your town centre and press Advance to the Keep Age (350 food, 120 gold) to unlock Watch Towers and Archers. The Crown Age (700 food, 350 gold) adds Knights and reinforces every tower. Locked buttons in the command card show what each age brings.

Goal

Destroy the Ashfell town centre to win. Lose your own town centre and the match is over. Villagers gather food, wood and gold. Houses raise the population limit, barracks train soldiers, and the town centre and watch towers shoot at raiders.

Paused

Press P to resume.
